Government Notifications & MoRTH Guidelines

Mandatory High Security Registration Plates (HSRP) Directive

As per Rule 50 of the Central Motor Vehicles Rules (CMVR), 1989 and Ministry of Road Transport and Highways (MoRTH) statutory orders, all motor vehicles registered prior to 1st April 2019 must be retrofitted with HSRP plates and Color-Coded Fuel Stickers.

Legal Enforcement & Challan Exemption Rules

Vehicle owners possessing a valid payment receipt and appointment slip from the authorized HSRP portal are exempted from traffic police enforcement challans during the period between booking and the scheduled fitment date.

About HSRP & Color-Coded Stickers

High Security Registration Plates (HSRP) are aluminium-made license plates featuring a chromium-based hot-stamped hologram of the Ashoka Chakra, a laser-etched 10-digit unique Laser Identification Number (LID), and non-reusable snap locks to prevent vehicle theft and counterfeiting.

Laser PIN Code

Unique 10-digit PIN linked to the national VAHAN database.

Snap Locks

Tamper-evident snap locks that break upon unauthorized removal attempts.

Fuel Color Sticker

Blue for Petrol/CNG, Orange for Diesel, and Light Green for EV vehicles.

Refund & Cancellation Policy

1. Order Cancellation:

Cancellations are allowed within 24 hours of booking or prior to the commencement of plate embossing in the manufacturing plant.

2. Refund Processing:

Once approved, refunds are credited back to the original payment source or provided bank/UPI account within 5–7 business days.

3. Non-Refundable Scenarios:

No refund shall be issued if incorrect vehicle data was entered by the user and manufacturing of the customized plate has been completed.

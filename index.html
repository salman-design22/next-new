Government Notifications & MoRTH Guidelines

Mandatory High Security Registration Plates (HSRP) Directive

As per Rule 50 of the Central Motor Vehicles Rules (CMVR), 1989 and Ministry of Road Transport and Highways (MoRTH) mandates, all motor vehicles registered prior to 1st April 2019 must be fitted with HSRP plates and Color Coded Fuel Stickers.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

What is HSRP?

High Security Registration Plate (HSRP) is an aluminium plate fitted with a chromium-based hot-stamped hologram and laser-etched 10-digit PIN number for anti-theft security.

How long does home fitment take?

Once booked, the plate goes into manufacturing and is delivered/fitted by an authorized technician within 5 to 7 business days.
